## [listpairreview.pl](https://github.com/wadehuber/codeexamples/blob/master/prolog/prolog3/listpairreview.pl)

A review of Prolog lists written for students coming from the Scheme module: shows how Prolog's `[Head | Tail]` structure corresponds to Scheme's pairs, with Prolog rules that mirror car, cdr, cadr, cons, & append.

Using Cut (!) to control backtracking in Prolog

## [notcasewithcut.pl](https://github.com/wadehuber/codeexamples/blob/master/prolog/prolog3/notcasewithcut.pl)

Shows how a cut lets the next clause act as the "else" branch so you don't have to restate (or negate) the condition you already tested.  Examples include finding the larger of two numbers, checking membership, and classifying by age.
